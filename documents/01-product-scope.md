# Product scope

## Objective

Enable organizations to run safe, accessible service events from discovery through measurable impact without creating separate applications per city, organization, or year.

## Users and outcomes

- **Visitors** discover public organizations, events, sites, accessibility information, and approved impact stories.
- **Volunteers** create an account, sign required documents, join alone or with a team, receive assignments, navigate, check in/out, and submit impact information.
- **Guardians** explicitly consent for named minors and control each minor's participation and media visibility.
- **Site leads** view only assigned operational details, attendance, tasks, incidents, and announcements.
- **Organizers** configure their tenant, events, sites, registration, communications, moderation, and reports.
- **Platform operators** support tenants and investigate abuse without routine access to tenant data.

## MVP

Native iPhone experience; responsive public registration and guardian-consent pages; organizer web console; organization onboarding; reusable events and dated occurrences; sites and capacity; individual/team registration; versioned waivers; assignments; maps/directions; check-in/out; announcements; private media upload and moderation; impact totals; audit trail; exports and account deletion.

## Explicitly out of scope for v1

Android native application, peer-to-peer direct messaging, public live volunteer tracking, automated facial identification, fundraising/payments, volunteer background-check adjudication, and autonomous safety decisions.

## Success measures

Track registration completion, waiver/consent completion, assignment coverage, check-in success, notification delivery, media moderation time, crash-free sessions, API latency, accessibility defects, privacy incidents, and organizer time to publish an event. Metrics must avoid precise location and unnecessary minor data.

## Constraints

The product must support multiple organizations and time zones, intermittent connectivity at crowded sites, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type, least-privileged access, data subject requests, and public App Store distribution.
