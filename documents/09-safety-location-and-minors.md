# Safety, location, and minors

## Safety model

The application supports coordination; it does not replace emergency services or trained supervision. Emergency screens prioritize calling local emergency services and contacting an on-site lead. Incident access is highly restricted, audited, and retained separately.

## Location

Directions require only foreground/on-demand location. Public site pages show only approved precision. Safety Sharing requires an explicit purpose, occurrence/site, authorized recipients, visible active indicator, short maximum duration, manual stop, and automatic expiry. It is never required for registration, never sold or used for advertising, never exposed publicly, and not retained as a movement history. Background permission requires a separately approved use case and review.

## Minors

- A named, authenticated guardian creates/manages each dependent and signs separately for that child.
- Verify relationship by attestation and escalation rules; do not imply stronger verification than performed.
- Minimize birth data; derive eligibility where possible and restrict raw values.
- Team leaders and peers do not gain guardian rights or private contact details.
- Minors cannot upload/publish media, direct message, activate location sharing, or alter consent.
- Guardian controls participation, emergency contact, pickup/authorization where applicable, and media visibility.
- Staff access is site/occurrence scoped and time limited.

## Abuse and escalation

Every public media item and relevant user surface offers report/block/support paths. Takedown can immediately unpublish while investigation continues. Threats, exploitation, missing-child concerns, and imminent danger follow documented severity playbooks and preserve evidence with restricted access.

## Data minimization

Never expose minor rosters publicly. Reports use aggregation/suppression thresholds. Logs and analytics exclude names, contacts, precise locations, free-text incidents, consent payloads, and media URLs.
